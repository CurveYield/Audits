# V18.9 Audit Fixes Design

M-01 is resolved with an additive rate-seconds decay accumulator. Principal index is derived from cumulative elapsed rate units against the epoch's original index, not multiplied by each checkpoint's remaining index. Reward accrual uses the integral of the linearly declining target rate, split if it crosses the constant max-rate cap. Mint top-ups and funding order remain unchanged.

M-02 is resolved with two retirement paths. Normal retirement strictly harvests, converts, compounds, forwards cyGOV, and then withdraws principal. Emergency retirement skips reward claims, permanently marks/pauses the strategy, withdraws principal, and is reachable only through a matured replacement candidate in the vault.

Revenue Staking excess fees are 33% Treasury and 7% live admin.
